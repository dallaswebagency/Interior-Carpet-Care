Meet Dwayne

One owner. Same hands on every job.

Dwayne Eberle started Interior Carpet Care in 1992 after working in the carpet cleaning divisions of two large department stores. He saw how the push to sell led to high prices and poor cleaning, so he built his business on reliable, quality work instead.

It is a family business. Some of his clients' kids have grown up to become clients themselves.
